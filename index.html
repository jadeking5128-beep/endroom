01 / THE DISAPPEARANCE

Long ago, the Sun Chalice kept the Hollow Kingdom warm. One moonless night it vanished from the royal hall, and the castle lights went dark. The old gates sealed themselves.

02 / THE QUEST

You are a little square of a knight, sent through winding halls and forgotten gardens. Three sleepy scale-keepers guard the paths. A curious winged thief has its own plans for anything you carry.

03 / THE SECRET

Legends mention a nearly invisible mark hidden where no mark should be. Find the odd pixel, carry its secret to the quiet chamber, and the kingdom may reveal who made it.
